# 📘 Day 5 — Task: Build the Week 4 Presentation

**Time window:** 6 hours (self-paced, spread across the day)
**Actual hands-on effort:** ~1 hour — light, wrap-up work. We're
assembling what already exists, not creating new content.

---

## 🎯 Objective
Turn the week's work into a 5-slide Canva presentation we could actually
show someone.

---

## ✅ Step-by-Step

### 1. Pick a Template (5 min)
Open Canva, choose one simple presentation template. Don't customize
fonts/colors — use what the template gives us.

### 2. Build the 5 Slides (40 min)
| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Title: pipeline name + one-sentence description |
| 2 | The problem: what was messy about the raw data (from Day 1) |
| 3 | The pipeline diagram (from Day 2 — reuse the PNG, don't redraw it) |
| 4 | One before/after example: a single row, raw vs. clean (from Day 2 Task B) |
| 5 | Takeaway: our proudest moment or biggest lesson (from Day 3 journal) |

### 3. Export (10 min)
Export as PDF or PNG images, save as `week4_presentation.pdf`.

---

## 📦 Deliverable
- `week4_presentation.pdf` (or exported slide images)

## 🧭 Definition of Done
- 5 slides, one idea per slide, no paragraphs of text
- Diagram and before/after example are reused, not rebuilt from scratch
- Presentation could be talked through out loud in under 3 minutes
