# 🧠 How businesses use data to guide actions ( 3 Hours )


So far, Lina has:
- Collected her lemonade sales data  
- Cleaned it in Google Sheets  
- Explored patterns using filters, pivot tables, and charts  

Now she asks:
> “What should I **do** with what I found?”

This is the final and most important step of working with data — using it to **make decisions**.

Data isn’t just about charts. It’s about **actions**: what to change, what to keep, what to stop.

<br>

## 💼 How Lina uses her insights

Let’s look at the actual decisions Lina makes after reviewing her data.

---

### ✅ Decision 1: Promote Lemon again next month

**Observation:** Lemon consistently sells the most cups, even when priced slightly higher.

**Action:**  
- She prepares double the lemon stock on weekends  
- She places a chalkboard sign saying:  
  _“Best Seller: Classic Lemon – Try it Today!”_

💡 *Data helps confirm what works — so she can do more of it.*

---

### ✅ Decision 2: Raise the Mint price slightly

**Observation:** After launching the new mint recipe on June 8, mint sales increased — even though the price was higher.

**Action:**  
- She raises the mint price by €0.2 (from 1.8 to 2.0)  
- Watches carefully to see if customers still buy it

💡 *Data supports trying new strategies with less risk.*

---

### ✅ Decision 3: Ask cousin to help on busy days

**Observation:** Saturdays and sunny Sundays have the highest cup counts.

**Action:**  
- She texts her cousin for help on weekends only  
- Uses the extra hands to speed up service and reduce wait time

💡 *Data helps her manage time and people better.*

---

## 🔁 From dashboard to boardroom

Lina’s lemonade stand may be small — but her decision-making is now **data-driven**.

The same process happens at large companies:

| Insight                         | Real-world decision                            |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Sales drop after ad ends       | Resume or redesign the campaign                |
| Product performs best in region A | Shift inventory and marketing budget         |
| Customers love a feature       | Make it permanent or expand it                |
| Staff overwhelmed on Fridays   | Adjust schedules or hire part-time help        |

Whether you're working with Google Sheets or a $10M BI platform — the **core idea is the same**:

**Use data to guide smart actions.**

---

## 📈 Visual: Decision-making cycle

```text
Data → Clean → Explore → Decide → Track → Adjust → (Repeat)
```
It’s not one-and-done. Lina will repeat this every month — and get better each time.

## 🧩 Reflection: Ask before you act

Whenever you're reviewing data, try these prompts:

- What patterns am I seeing?  
- What might be causing them?  
- Is there something I can test?  
- What decision could I try?  
- How will I track if it worked?
