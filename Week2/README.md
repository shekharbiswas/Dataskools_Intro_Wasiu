# 📊 Week 2: Foundation of _data analysis_

Now that you’ve built confidence using key tools, it’s time to explore the world of **data and decision-making**.

This week is designed to help you:  
- Understand how raw data becomes real-world insights  
- Learn how modern teams use data at every step  
- Start asking better questions — like an analyst  
- Prepare for deeper work in Power BI, SQL, and Python (coming soon!)

<br>

## 📊 Effort Chart: Week 2 Overview

| Day     | Title                        | Description                                                                  | Effort Level     |
|---------|------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------|
| Day 1   | Data roles & value chain     | Understand who does what in data, and how raw data becomes business insight  | ⭐ ⭐ Light           |
| Day 2   | Collecting & organizing      | Learn where data comes from and how it’s stored, moved, and accessed         | ⭐⭐ Light         |
| Day 3   | Cleaning, exploration & use  | Clean messy data, explore patterns, and make sense of numbers                | ⭐⭐⭐ Medium-High  |
| Day 4   | Thinking like an analyst     | Practice making decisions, evaluating impact, and applying ethical thinking  | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ High        |

💡 You’ll start with light exploration and build up to real-world analysis and reflection — just like a growing analyst would.
