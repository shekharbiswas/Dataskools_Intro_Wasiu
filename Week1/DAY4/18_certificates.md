# 🎓Certificates: what you earn and how to show your progress

Certificates aren’t the main goal — but they help you **mark your journey** and **show the world what you’ve done**.

At Dataskools, we offer 2 types of certificates.  
They are awarded based on your commitment, learning progress, and final submission.

<br>

## 🥇 1. Certificate of completion

This is the one we want you to aim for.  
It means you’ve:

- Finished **all the modules**
- Participated in the **mock interviews**
- Shared your **final project or reflection**
- Maintained a _consistent_ presence throughout the course

This certificate is personalized, signed, and shareable on LinkedIn, GitHub, or your résumé.

> 🏆 This is the certificate most aligned with job readiness.

<br>

## 🎓 2. Certificate of participation

This is for students who made progress but were **unable to complete all modules**.

It shows that you were part of the course, attended sessions, and did partial work — but didn’t finish all outcomes.

We offer this to recognize effort, but note:  
> This has **less weight** in portfolios or job applications.

That’s why we encourage you to **reach for the full completion certificate** — and we’re here to help you get there.

<br>

## 💼 What about Coursera, Tableau, or cloud certificates?

You may hear us mention external tools like:

- Coursera specializations  
- Tableau visualizations  
- Azure, AWS, or cloud certifications  

Here’s how you can achieve those:

These are **not included by default** — but they are **supported** by us.

When we promote these opportunities, it means:
- Our mentors will help you prepare  
- We’ll guide you on what to focus on  
- You’ll be part of optional learning paths (usually in advanced weeks)

> 🔑 You need to put in extra effort — and in some cases, pay a small fee — to unlock external certificates.

We do this because we want you to be **ready for industry standards**, not just internal success.

And many students **do** earn those badges, thanks to 1:1 support from mentors.

<br>

## 🌟 How to showcase your certificates

Once you’ve received a certificate:
- Upload it to LinkedIn under "Licenses & Certifications"
- Add a dedicated `certificates/` folder to your GitHub
- Write a short reflection: *“What I learned. What I built. What I want to do next.”*
- Mention it in interviews as proof of commitment and learning

> 📌 A certificate is proof of what you’ve finished.  
> A portfolio is proof of what you can do.

We help you build both.

<br>

## 🔁 Final reminder

We don’t measure you by certificates — we measure you by your growth.  
But if a certificate helps you **move forward**, we’ll do everything we can to help you earn one.

> 🎯 Your job is to keep showing up.  
> Our job is to keep guiding you — until you achieved what you wanted.
