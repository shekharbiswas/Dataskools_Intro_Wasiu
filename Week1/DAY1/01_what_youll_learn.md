## 🧭 What This Course Covers

This is a 24-week journey designed for **curious learners from any background** — career changers, returners, creatives, freelancers, or total beginners. Here's what you'll explore overall:

| Module                           | Focus Areas                                                                 |
|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Week 1–4: Intro to Data & AI     | Tools, systems, real-world use cases, foundational thinking                 |
| Week 5–8: Power BI               | Dashboards, business storytelling, AI visuals                               |

## 🤝 How We'll Guide You

We believe that learning is most powerful when it’s **clear, supported, and personal**. Here’s how you’ll be supported throughout:

### 🗓️ Weekly Live Sessions
- Hosted via **Zoom**.
- A chance to **ask questions**, go deeper, and get insights from **mentors who work in the industry**.
- Sometimes, you’ll be joined by someone who recently got hired — to share how they made it.

### 📂 Guided Learning with GitHub LMS with a Mentor
- Every week has a folder.
- Each folder contains **Markdown files**, exercises, links, and tools.
- Learn at your own pace — and **track your progress** visibly.

### 🔁 Reflection & Feedback Loops
- At the end of each week, you’ll reflect: what felt clear? What’s still confusing?
- We’ll use a simple tool called the **Comfortability Index**.
- You’ll also write a small note to your future self ✍️

<br>

## 🌱 Growth Happens Gradually

You don’t need to know everything. You just need to **keep showing up** — one day, one task, one question at a time.

Many of our learners began with thoughts like:

- “I’ve never used GitHub before…”
- “Everyone else seems ahead…”
- “What if I’m not good at this?”

These thoughts are completely normal.  
But here’s what we’ve seen again and again:  
The learners who grow are not the ones who **know the most** — they’re the ones who **keep trying**, **ask questions**, and **reflect on their progress**.


<br>


### 📈 What Growth *Actually* Looks Like

| Early Stage                 | Later Stage                        |
|----------------------------|------------------------------------|
| “I don’t understand this yet.”  | “Let me break it down step by step.” |
| “I feel behind.”                 | “We’re all on different journeys.”   |
| “This is overwhelming.”          | “I’ll focus on one small win today.” |

Notice the shift? It’s not about being perfect — it’s about changing your self-talk and trusting the process.
