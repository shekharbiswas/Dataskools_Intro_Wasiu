# 🗓️ Google Calendar Setup


In this course, time management is your secret superpower.  
That’s why we use **Google Calendar** to keep everything clear, organized, and stress-free.

You’ll get calendar invites for:
- 🧑‍🏫 Weekly live sessions (via Zoom)
- 📅 Check-ins and reflection reminders
- 🧠 Optional practice or co-working sessions

<br>

## 🧭 Why Google Calendar?

Google Calendar is a free tool that lets you:
- See all your upcoming sessions at a glance  
- Get automatic reminders before live classes  
- Avoid missing important events  
- Plan your learning hours around your life  

This helps you **build a rhythm**, even if you have a busy schedule or other responsibilities.

> ⏰ Consistency beats intensity. Planning helps you show up, even on low-energy days.

<br>

## ✅ How to Accept Invites

Once you’re added to the calendar, you’ll receive email invites for each session.

Here’s what to do:
1. Open the email from “Dataskools Session”  
2. Click **“Yes”** to accept the invite  
3. The session will automatically appear in your calendar  
4. Click the event for details, Zoom link, and session info  

> 📌 You’ll need a Google account to access this easily. If you don’t have one yet, [create a Google account here](https://accounts.google.com/signup).

<br>

## 🔗 How to Join a Session

Each calendar event will include a Zoom link.  
When the session time arrives:

1. Open your Google Calendar  
2. Click on the session  
3. Click the Zoom link  
4. You’re in! 🎉

Pro tip: Turn on calendar notifications so you get reminders 10 minutes before each session.

<br>

## 🧠 Build Your Weekly Routine

You’re not just learning content — you’re learning how to **make time for your growth**.

Try this:
- Block off 3 short “learning windows” in your week (even 25–45 mins)
- Add personal notes like “Read Slack updates” or “Do reflection”
- Treat your calendar as your **accountability partner**, not the office boss.

This is how professionals plan their growth — and now, so you will.
