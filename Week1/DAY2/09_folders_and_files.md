# 🗂️ Folders and Files: organizing your digital workspace

Before we dive deeper into learning tools, let’s start with a habit that can save you time, stress, and confusion:  
**keeping your digital workspace organized**.

You don’t need to be technical to do this. In fact, the most successful learners and professionals are those who manage their files with intention.

<br>

## 📁 What are Folders and Files?

Let’s keep it simple:

- A **file** is a digital document — like a note, image, code file, or video. Examples: `notes.txt`, `photo.jpg`, `budget.xlsx`, `report.docx`
- A **folder** is like a container or drawer on your computer that holds files (and sometimes other folders)

You use folders to group related files together and keep things clean.

> 🎯 Think of it like organizing a physical binder — each subject has its own section.

<br>

## 💡 Why folder structure matters

Imagine working on this course for 4 weeks and downloading multiple documents, screenshots, and notes. 

If everything goes to your **Downloads folder** or **desktop**, it will quickly become overwhelming.

Now imagine having this instead:

<pre>
  
Dataskools/
├── Week-1/
│ ├── 01_my_notes.md
│ ├── my-goals.txt
├── Week-2/
│ ├── calendar_practice.png
│ └── slack_notes.docx

</pre>


This simple folder structure helps you:
- Find what you need quickly
- Avoid version confusion like `final_version_edited2_notes.txt`
- Feel organized and confident while working

> 🧠 When your files are clean, your mind is calmer too.

<br>

## 🧭 How to Organize Your Course Files

Here’s what we recommend for this course:

### 1. Create a Main Folder  
Name it something like `Dataskools`, `AI-Course`, or `Learning-Journal`.

### 2. Add Weekly Subfolders  
Inside your main folder, create:
- `Week-1`
- `Week-2`
- `Week-3`
- etc.

Put all your notes, screenshots, and assignments for that week inside the matching folder.

### 3. Name Files Clearly  
Use names that describe the file. Examples:
- ✅ `03_google_calendar_notes.md`
- ✅ `zoom_test_screenshot.png`
- ✅ `week2_reflection.txt`

Avoid:
- ❌ `finalfinal_notes.docx`
- ❌ `untitled_3.txt`

### 4. Clean Regularly  
Once a week, check your desktop or downloads folder and move relevant files into the right place. Delete what you don’t need.

<br>

## 🧘‍♀️ Why This Helps You

- You’ll save time and avoid frustration
- You’ll build confidence as you start seeing your learning grow folder by folder
- You’ll stay focused — instead of searching for "that one file you swear you saved"
- You’ll be ready for more advanced tools later (like GitHub or sharing work)

> 🔑 Good digital habits now = better productivity in every future project
